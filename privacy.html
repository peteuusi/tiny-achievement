# Privacy Policy — Bureau of Tiny Achievements **Last updated: November 18, 2026** **Developer:** Petri Uusitalo ## 1. Introduction Bureau of Tiny Achievements respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android application. ## 2. Information Collection Bureau of Tiny Achievements is designed to operate offline. The app does not intentionally collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers. ## 3. Local Data Storage The app stores achievement progress and preferences locally on your device. This information is used to maintain your achievements between sessions and is not transmitted to the developer. ## 4. Advertising and Analytics The app does not include third-party advertisements, analytics services, or user tracking. ## 5. Certificates and Sharing Users can generate achievement certificates and choose to save or share them using Android's built-in functionality. Sharing is initiated by the user. Information shared with external applications is subject to those applications' privacy policies. ## 6. Children's Privacy The app is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 7. Data Retention Locally stored achievement progress generally remains on the device until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the application. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This privacy policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data-handling practices change. ## 9.
