Privacy Policy

Bureau of Tiny Achievements

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Bureau of Tiny Achievements is an Android application developed by Petri Uusitalo. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the application simple and transparent.

2. Information Collection

The application is designed to work offline. It does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the developer or external servers.

3. Local Data Storage

Achievement progress and application preferences are stored locally on your device. This allows your achievements to remain available between sessions.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The application does not include third-party advertisements, analytics services, or user tracking.

5. Certificates and Sharing

Users may generate, save, and share achievement certificates. Sharing is initiated by the user through Android's sharing functionality. External applications may process shared information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Achievement progress is stored locally on the device. Users can remove locally stored application data through Android's application settings. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local application data, subject to any device backup and restore functionality.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
